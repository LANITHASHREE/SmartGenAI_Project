    How we estimate cost (methodology)

    Every picture shows an estimated cost and the saving versus the maximum setting ({{ presets.poster.label }}, {{ presets.poster.size }}px, {{ presets.poster.steps }} steps, what the district used for every request). We cannot meter the GPU, so the numbers come from one transparent formula, applied the same way to smart and maximum:

    Compute units(size ÷ 512)² × steps   (maximum picture = {{ "%g"|format(method.max_units) }} units)
    Energy (Wh)units × {{ "%.4f"|format(method.wh) }} Wh   calibrated: a 1024px / 30-step picture ≈ 2.9 Wh (about 3 kWh per 1000 images in published diffusion measurements)
    Time (s)units × {{ "%.3f"|format(method.sec) }} s   about 4 s for 1024px / 30 steps on a mid-range GPU; the real stopwatch time is also shown
    CO₂ (g)energy × {{ method.co2 }} g/Wh   India grid, about 0.7 kg per kWh
    Library reusefixed {{ method.lookup }} Wh for reading a stored picture
    Savingmaximum − smart. With drafts, the maximum assumes every try would have been a full picture.

    To use measured hardware numbers, change WH_PER_COMPUTE_UNIT and SEC_PER_COMPUTE_UNIT in app.py.
